# Computer Science: An Overview (13th Edition)

# Study Note

# Chapter 1: Data Storage

# 第1章 数据存储

# 1.1 Bits and Their Storage

# 1.1 位及其存储

Abstractions allow us to represent many kinds of data, but at the lowest level, inside today's computers all information is encoded as patterns of 0s and 1s. These digits are called bits (short for binary digits).

抽象使我们能够表示多种类型的数据，但在最底层，在当今的计算机内部，所有信息都编码为 0 和 1 的模式。这些数字被称为位 (bits)（是 binary digits [二进制数字] 的缩写）。

Although you may be inclined to associate bits with numeric values, they are really just symbols whose meaning depends on the application at hand.

虽然你可能倾向于将位与数值联系起来，但它们实际上只是符号，其含义取决于当前的应用。

To understand how individual bits are stored within a computer, we must envision a device that can be in one of two states, such as a switch being on or off, or a magnet's field pointing in one direction or the other.

为了理解单个位是如何在计算机内部存储的，我们必须设想一种能够处于两种状态之一的设备，例如处于开或关状态的开关，或者磁场指向一个方向或另一个方向的磁体。

We can represent the two states of such a device by the digits 0 and 1. Thus, to store a bit, we set the state of the device to correspond to the value of the bit. To retrieve the bit, we look at the device to see what state it is in.

我们可以用数字 0 和 1 来表示这种设备的两种状态。因此，要存储一个位，我们将设备的状态设置为对应于该位的值。要检索该位，我们查看设备处于什么状态。

A variety of electronic circuits (such as flip-flops, which we will describe shortly) are used to store bits in a computer's main memory. Mass storage devices (such as hard disk drives and flash drives) utilize magnetism and electric charge to store bits.

各种电子电路（例如我们要简短描述的触发器）被用于在计算机的主存储器 (main memory) 中存储位。海量存储器 (mass storage) 设备（如硬盘驱动器和闪存驱动器）利用磁性和电荷来存储位。

# Boolean Operations

# 布尔运算

To understand how individual bits are stored and manipulated inside a computer, it is convenient to imagine that the bit 0 represents the value false and the bit 1 represents the value true. Operations that manipulate true/false values are called Boolean operations .

为了理解单个位如何在计算机内部被存储和操作，将位 0 想象为表示值“假 (false)”，将位 1 想象为表示值“真 (true)”是很方便的。操作真/假值的运算称为布尔运算 (Boolean operations)。

Three of the basic Boolean operations are AND, OR, and XOR (exclusive or). These operations are similar to the arithmetic operations times and plus because they combine a pair of values (the operation's input) to produce a third value (the output).

三个基本的布尔运算是 AND (与)、OR (或) 和 XOR (异或)。这些运算类似于算术运算中的乘法和加法，因为它们结合一对值（运算的输入）来产生第三个值（输出）。

In contrast to arithmetic operations, the only values involved in Boolean operations are true and false.

与算术运算不同，布尔运算涉及的唯一值是真和假。

The AND operation is designed to reflect the truth or falseness of a compound statement formed by joining two smaller statements with the conjunction and. Such a compound statement is true only if both of its components are true.

AND 运算旨在反映由连词 and 连接两个较小陈述而形成的复合陈述的真假。这样的复合陈述只有在其两个组成部分都为真时才为真。

Consequently, the input to the AND operation represents the truth or falseness of the compound statement's components; the output represents the truth or falseness of the compound statement itself. Thus, 1 AND 11 \text{ AND } 11 AND 1 produces 111, whereas 1 AND 01 \text{ AND } 01 AND 0 produces 000. (1 represents true; 0 represents false.)

因此，AND 运算的输入表示复合陈述组成部分的真假；输出表示复合陈述本身的真假。因此，1 AND 11 \text{ AND } 11 AND 1 产生 111，而 1 AND 01 \text{ AND } 01 AND 0 产生 000。（1 表示真；0 表示假。）

The OR operation is based on compound statements comprising two smaller statements joined by the conjunction or. In the English language, the term or is sometimes used to mean "either one or the other, but not both" and other times to mean "either one or the other, or both."

OR 运算基于由连词 or 连接两个较小陈述组成的复合陈述。在英语中，术语 or 有时用来表示“两者之一，但不是两者都”（异或），有时用来表示“两者之一，或者是两者都”（包含或）。

The Boolean operation OR is based on the latter meaning. It produces an output of 1 (true) when at least one of its inputs is 1 (true).

布尔运算 OR 基于后一种含义。当其输入中至少有一个为 1（真）时，它产生输出 1（真）。

The operation XOR (exclusive or) produces an output of 1 (true) when one of its inputs is 1 (true) and the other is 0 (false). (See Figure 1.1.)

运算 XOR (异或) 当其输入中有一个为 1（真）而另一个为 0（假）时，产生输出 1（真）。（见图 1.1。）

Another common Boolean operation is the NOT operation, which differs from AND, OR, and XOR because it has only one input. Its output is the opposite of that input; if the input is 1 (true), the output is 0 (false), and vice versa.

另一个常见的布尔运算是 NOT (非) 运算，它与 AND、OR 和 XOR 不同，因为它只有一个输入。其输出是该输入的反面；如果输入是 1（真），输出是 0（假），反之亦然。

Figure 1.1 The possible input and output values of Boolean operations AND, OR, and XOR (exclusive or)

图 1.1 布尔运算 AND、OR 和 XOR (异或) 可能的输入和输出值

# Gates

# 门

A device that produces the output of a Boolean operation when given the operation's input values is called a gate. Gates can be constructed from a variety of technologies, such as gears, relays, and optic devices.

当给定布尔运算的输入值时，能够产生该运算输出的设备称为门 (gate)。门可以由多种技术构建，如齿轮、继电器和光学器件。

Figure 1.2 shows the standard symbols used to represent gates in circuit diagrams. Note that the symbols for AND and OR are similar. The AND symbol is roughly D-shaped, whereas the OR symbol is more pointed. The symbol for XOR is similar to the OR symbol but has an additional curved line on the input side.

图 1.2 显示了在电路图中用来表示门的标准符号。请注意，AND 和 OR 的符号很相似。AND 符号大致呈 D 形，而 OR 符号更尖一些（呈盾形）。XOR 的符号与 OR 符号相似，但在输入侧多了一条曲线。

Finally, the symbol for the NOT operation is a triangle with a small circle. This circle is often used in circuit diagrams to represent the NOT operation (inversion). For instance, the output of an AND gate can be negated (inverted) by placing a circle at its output.

最后，NOT 运算的符号是一个带小圆圈的三角形。这个圆圈在电路图中常用来表示 NOT 运算（反转）。例如，可以通过在 AND 门的输出端放置一个圆圈来对其输出进行取反（反转）。

The resulting structure is called a NAND gate (short for NOT-AND). Similarly, a NOR gate is an OR gate with its output inverted, and an XNOR gate is an XOR gate with its output inverted.

由此产生的结构称为 NAND 门（与非门，NOT-AND 的缩写）。类似地，NOR 门（或非门）是输出被反转的 OR 门，而 XNOR 门（同或门）是输出被反转的 XOR 门。

Gates provide the building blocks from which computers are constructed. As a concrete example, let us confirm that the circuit in Figure 1.3 is capable of storing a bit.

门提供了构建计算机的积木。作为一个具体的例子，让我们确认图 1.3 中的电路能够存储一个位。

The circuit is technically known as a flip-flop. A flip-flop is a fundamental unit of computer memory. It produces an output value of either 0 or 1, which remains constant until a temporary pulse from another circuit causes it to shift to the other value.

该电路在技术上被称为触发器 (flip-flop)。触发器是计算机存储器的基本单元。它产生 0 或 1 的输出值，该值保持不变，直到来自另一个电路的临时脉冲使其切换到另一个值。

In other words, the output will "flip" or "flop" between two values under control of external stimuli.

换句话说，在外部刺激的控制下，输出会在两个值之间“翻转 (flip)”或“改变 (flop)”。

# Flip-Flops

# 触发器

The flip-flop in Figure 1.3 is constructed from two NAND gates. Note that the output of each gate is connected to one of the inputs of the other. The remaining inputs (labeled "Upper input" and "Lower input") are used to control the flip-flop.

图 1.3 中的触发器由两个 NAND 门构成。请注意，每个门的输出都连接到另一个门的一个输入端。剩余的输入端（标记为“上输入”和“下输入”）用于控制触发器。

Normally both of these inputs are held at 1. As long as this is the case, the flip-flop maintains its current output state. However, by temporarily changing the upper input to 0, we can force the output to be 1. Or, by temporarily changing the lower input to 0, we can force the output to be 0.

通常，这两个输入都保持为 1。只要情况如此，触发器就维持其当前的输出状态。然而，通过暂时将上输入变为 0，我们可以强制输出为 1。或者，通过暂时将下输入变为 0，我们可以强制输出为 0。

Let's verify these claims. First, recall that the output of a NAND gate is 0 only if both of its inputs are 1; otherwise, the output is 1. Thus, if one of the inputs to a NAND gate is 0, the output will be 1 regardless of the other input.

让我们验证这些说法。首先，回想一下，只有当 NAND 门的两个输入都为 1 时，其输出才为 0；否则，输出为 1。因此，如果 NAND 门的一个输入为 0，无论另一个输入是什么，输出都将是 1。

Figure 1.3 A simple flip-flop circuit

图 1.3 一个简单的触发器电路

Now consider the case in which the upper input is 0 and the lower input is 1 (Figure 1.4a). Because the upper input is 0, the output of the top NAND gate must be 1. This 1 is fed back to the bottom NAND gate, so both inputs to the bottom gate are 1. Consequently, the output of the bottom gate is 0.

现在考虑上输入为 0 且下输入为 1 的情况（图 1.4a）。因为上输入为 0，顶部 NAND 门的输出必须为 1。这个 1 被反馈到底部 NAND 门，因此底部门的两个输入都是 1。结果，底部门的输出为 0。

This 0 is, in turn, fed back to the top gate, but since the upper input is already 0, this has no effect—the output of the top gate remains 1. In summary, setting the upper input to 0 forces the flip-flop's output to be 1.

这个 0 随后被反馈回顶部门，但由于上输入已经是 0，这没有影响——顶部门的输出保持为 1。总之，将上输入设置为 0 会强制触发器的输出为 1。

Once the output is established as 1, we can return the upper input to its normal value of 1, and the output will remain 1 (Figure 1.4b). This is because the 0 from the bottom gate is still being fed back to the top gate, which causes the output of the top gate to remain 1 even after the upper input is changed to 1.

一旦输出确立为 1，我们可以将上输入恢复为其正常值 1，输出将保持为 1（图 1.4b）。这是因为来自底部门的 0 仍然被反馈回顶部门，这导致即使在上输入变为 1 之后，顶部门的输出仍保持为 1。

In a similar manner, temporarily changing the lower input to 0 will force the output of the bottom gate to be 1 (Figure 1.4c). This 1 is fed back to the top gate (where the other input is 1), causing the output of the top gate to become 0. This 0 is then fed back to the bottom gate, which reinforces the 1 that the bottom gate is already producing.

以类似的方式，暂时将下输入变为 0 将强制底部门的输出为 1（图 1.4c）。这个 1 被反馈回顶部门（那里另一个输入是 1），导致顶部门的输出变为 0。这个 0 随后被反馈回底部门，加强了底部门已经产生的 1。

Thus, when the lower input is returned to 1, the output of the flip-flop remains 0 (Figure 1.4d).

因此，当下输入恢复为 1 时，触发器的输出保持为 0（图 1.4d）。

In summary, we have a circuit that can be used to store a bit. Placing a 0 on the upper input line forces the stored value to be 1, whereas placing a 0 on the lower input line forces the stored value to be 0.

总之，我们有了一个可以用来存储一个位的电路。在上输入线上放置 0 会强制存储的值为 1，而在下输入线上放置 0 会强制存储的值为 0。

When both inputs are 1, the circuit merely preserves the value that was previously stored. (We should note that placing a 0 on both input lines simultaneously is a problem because it forces the output of both gates to be 1—a state that is unstable because returning the inputs to 1 causes the final state of the flip-flop to depend on which input reaches 1 first. Thus, the flip-flop in Figure 1.3 is designed to be used in an environment where the inputs are never 0 at the same time.)

当两个输入都为 1 时，电路仅仅保留之前存储的值。（我们应该注意，同时在两条输入线上放置 0 是个问题，因为它会强制两个门的输出都为 1——这是一种不稳定的状态，因为将输入恢复为 1 会导致触发器的最终状态取决于哪个输入先达到 1。因此，图 1.3 中的触发器设计用于输入永远不会同时为 0 的环境中。）

Our purpose in presenting the flip-flop circuit is not to prepare you to design circuits but rather to demonstrate how devices can be constructed from gates and to show that the abstract concept of storing a bit can be implemented with current technology.

我们展示触发器电路的目的不是为了让你准备好设计电路，而是为了演示如何用门构建设备，并表明存储一个位的抽象概念可以用现有技术实现。

Another way of constructing a flip-flop is shown in Figure 1.5. This circuit is constructed from different gates than the previous one, and it is controlled by different input patterns. However, it is still able to store a bit.

另一种构建触发器的方法如图 1.5 所示。该电路是用与前一个不同的门构建的，并且由不同的输入模式控制。然而，它仍然能够存储一个位。

Engineers have developed many such circuits, each with its own properties. The definition of a flip-flop is general enough to encompass all these circuits: A flip-flop is a circuit that produces an output value of 0 or 1, which remains constant until a pulse from another circuit causes it to shift to the other value.

工程师们已经开发了许多这样的电路，每种都有其自己的特性。触发器的定义足够通用，可以包含所有这些电路：触发器是一种产生 0 或 1 输出值的电路，该值保持不变，直到来自另一个电路的脉冲使其切换到另一个值。

The abstract tool "flip-flop" is a perfect example of abstraction in computer science. A computer designer can use flip-flops to build larger components (such as main memory) without worrying about the internal details of the flip-flops themselves. The designer simply assumes that the flip-flops work as advertised.

抽象工具“触发器”是计算机科学中抽象的一个完美例子。计算机设计者可以使用触发器来构建更大的组件（如主存储器），而无需担心触发器本身的内部细节。设计者只需假设触发器如所宣传的那样工作。

# Hexadecimal Notation

# 十六进制记数法

When considering the internal activities of a computer, we must deal with strings of bits, which can be quite long. A stream of 0s and 1s such as

在考虑计算机的内部活动时，我们必须处理位串，这些位串可能相当长。一串 0 和 1，例如：

101101010011101101010011 101101010011

is difficult for a human mind to comprehend. To simplify the representation of such bit patterns, we usually use a shorthand notation called hexadecimal notation.

对于人类思维来说很难理解。为了简化这种位模式的表示，我们通常使用一种称为十六进制记数法 (hexadecimal notation) 的简写符号。

This notation takes advantage of the fact that bit patterns within a computer usually have lengths that are multiples of four. In particular, hexadecimal notation uses a single symbol to represent a pattern of four bits.

这种记数法利用了这样一个事实：计算机内部的位模式长度通常是 4 的倍数。特别地，十六进制记数法使用单个符号来表示 4 个位的模式。

Figure 1.6 shows the table of hexadecimal numerals and the bit patterns they represent.

图 1.6 显示了十六进制数字及其所表示的位模式的表格。

# Essential Knowledge Statements

# 基本知识陈述

The basics of digital electronics are based on the fact that a light switch is either on or off.

数字电子学的基础基于这样一个事实：电灯开关要么是开的，要么是关的。

The fundamental building block of digital electronics is the gate.

数字电子学的基本构建块是门 (gate)。

The four basic Boolean operations are AND, OR, XOR, and NOT.

四种基本的布尔运算是 AND (与)、OR (或)、XOR (异或) 和 NOT (非)。

Hexadecimal notation is a shorthand used to represent long bit patterns.

十六进制记数法是一种用于表示长位模式的简写形式。

    假设图 1.3 中触发器的“上输入”是 1，“下输入”是 0。输出的状态是什么？
